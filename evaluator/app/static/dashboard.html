Where the requests went

Read it left to right. The band is every request that came in; its thickness is how many. What a guardrail stops falls away below it. What is left on the right reached the model.

Each guardrail

A guardrail is a checkpoint in front of the model. These are measurements of what it did, not a verdict on whether it was right.

Over time

Each bar is a slice of the window. A colour that suddenly grows is something starting to happen: an attack, a guardrail slowing down, or a rule catching more than before.

Worth a look

Patterns in the numbers that deserve a person's attention. Each one says what was seen, what it could mean, and what to check. None of them proves a fault.

Requests, one by one

What the words mean
Request
One question sent to the AI model through the gateway.
Guardrail
A checkpoint that reads each request before the model sees it and can stop it.
Stopped
The gateway refused the request. It never reached the model.
Waited, then let through
The guardrail took about as long as its time limit and the request went on. This is what a guardrail that fails open looks like from outside, so the request may not have been checked. It can also be a guardrail that was simply slow and did answer.
Time in the guardrail
How long the checkpoint held a request. "Typical" is the middle request; "slowest 5%" is what the unlucky ones saw.
Tried again and got through
A caller was stopped, changed the wording, and passed. Either an honest person rephrasing after a wrong stop, or someone stepping around the rule. Reading a few of those requests tells you which.
Window
All numbers cover the last few minutes or hours you picked at the top, and slide forward as time passes.

Connect your Kong

One command on a machine that can reach your Kong's Admin API. It adds three plugins that already ship with Kong (nothing to install or build) to one route, and they report each request's outcome and timing here. Prompts are not sent. Your guardrail is not changed. koe disconnect removes everything it added.
